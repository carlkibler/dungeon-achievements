About this generator

Dungeon Achievements takes something you did, such as beating a deadline, eating cereal for dinner, or surviving a meeting, and gives you three fake RPG achievements. Each one has a title, description, and reward. The writing mimics the sarcastic System AI from Dungeon Crawler Carl, Matt Dinniman's LitRPG series commonly shortened to DCC. Choose default snark, corporate, absurd, wholesome, brutal, pirate, or Shakespearean bard. It is free, needs no account, and stores recent achievements only in your browser.

Questions the System has anticipated

What does DCC stand for?

DCC is fan shorthand for Dungeon Crawler Carl, Matt Dinniman's LitRPG series in which a snarky AI narrates a deadly dungeon and hands out absurd achievements to the crawlers inside it. That AI's voice is the one this generator imitates.

Who narrates the Dungeon Crawler Carl audiobooks?

Jeff Hays narrates every book in the series, voicing Carl, Donut, and the System AI. He is also the founder of Soundbooth Theater, the studio that publishes the audiobooks and produces the full-cast Audio Immersion Tunnel adaptation.

Are these real Dungeon Crawler Carl achievements?

No. Every achievement here is newly generated for whatever you typed. Nothing is copied from the books. The goal is the voice, not the canon.

Is this official or affiliated with Matt Dinniman?

No. This is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or licensed from Matt Dinniman or the publishers of Dungeon Crawler Carl. If you found this before you found the books, fix that: start with book one.

Is it free?

Yes. No signup, no account, no payment. Close the tab and the System forgets you, which is more than it does in the books.

Colophon
